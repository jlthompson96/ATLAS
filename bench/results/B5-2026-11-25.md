No injection case produced a proposal or any write. All 9 planner injection misses and reader misses are listed above: two harmless reads and one unflagged item.

## Other observations

- **Gemma 4 E4B thinks before it answers.** On the 2K-token prompt it spent all 320 tokens reasoning (317 reasoning tokens) and never reached visible text. That hurts a voice assistant: reasoning counts against the 1,024-token per-call cap and the 60 s request budget, and explains the planner step p95 of 14 s. Whether LM Studio can turn reasoning off for this model is not yet checked.
- Prefill is fast (539 ms at 2K tokens) and decode is 49 tok/s, far inside the criteria, with 2.8 GB of VRAM to spare at 16K context.
